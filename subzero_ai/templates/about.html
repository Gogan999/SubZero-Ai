Using {{ settings.app_name }} responsibly

{{ settings.app_name }} lets Team {{ settings.team_number }} students ask Claude for help with robot code, CAD, fabrication, physics, and materials, with a mentor checking every message first.

What it's for

Use it to understand things: why code behaves the way it does, how a mechanism works, which material fits a part, how to check a calculation. Claude is set up to teach, so expect explanations and questions back, not just finished answers.

How approval works

  • When you send a message, it waits for a mentor. You'll get a Slack DM when they act on it.
  • A mentor can approve it, edit it before Claude sees it, reject it with a reason, or follow up with you in person.
  • Every follow-up message needs its own approval.
  • Mentors' own messages go straight to Claude.

Who can see what

  • Approved messages and Claude's replies are visible to everyone on the team.
  • Messages waiting for review, and rejected ones, are visible only to you and the mentors.
  • Nothing here is a private conversation. Don't share personal information like your address or phone number.
  • Your chats are stored on the team's server. Anthropic, which makes Claude, normally deletes the messages it receives within 30 days and doesn't use them to train its models.

Check before you trust it

Claude can be wrong. Confirm load ratings, material limits, wiring, and anything else safety-critical with a mentor before it goes on the robot. Never use Claude's answer as a reason to skip shop safety rules.

If something's wrong

If you're going through something hard, talk to a parent, a mentor, or your school counselor. In the US you can call or text 988 any time, and call 911 in an emergency.
